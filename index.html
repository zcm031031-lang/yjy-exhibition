About Us

Yangzhou Yijiayou Hotel Supplies Co., Ltd.

Founded in 2015 (formerly Yongxin Plastics Factory), our headquarters and 4,000 m² plant are located in Hangji Town, Yangzhou, Jiangsu — China's famous hotel-supplies manufacturing hub — with a 6,000 m² branch facility in Tongcheng, Anhui. Together, over 120 employees support stable, large-scale production.

We specialize in garbage bags, laundry bags, gift bags, shopping bags and non-woven bags, supplying hotels, resorts, property management companies and households. Guided by our principles — Quality First, Reputation First, Customer Supreme — we warmly welcome partners from around the world.
